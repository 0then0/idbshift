# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in IDBShift issues, pull requests, and discussions is expected to treat others respectfully, welcome constructive corrections, and support technical disagreements with evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate exposure of private information, and persistent disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation proportionately, considering its context and impact.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact route on the [repository owner's profile](https://github.com/0then0). Do not post identifying or sensitive details in a public issue. If a private contact route is unavailable, GitHub's report-abuse channel can be used instead.

Maintainers should protect private information, acknowledge reports, assess the available evidence, and communicate an appropriate resolution privately. Enforcement must follow the same standards of respectful conduct.
